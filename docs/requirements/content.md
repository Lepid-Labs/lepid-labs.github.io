# Content requirements

What the site says, what it leaves out, and how it presents Lepid Labs.

## RQ-001 The site presents a software lab, not a Weft company

Status: agreed

Weft is the first release of a wide portfolio (developer tooling, knowledge and agents, platform services, creative
tools). The home page leads with the lab's line and method, then Weft, then the pipeline by area. It MUST NOT be
rewritten as a product landing page, and MUST NOT embed Weft; the FAQ and Docs pages already demonstrate it.

## RQ-002 The pipeline is listed by area, never by repository

Status: agreed

A project gets a name on the site only when it is usable by someone other than the lab. Hobby-grade work in the GitHub
organisation (bots, a torrent client) stays off the site.

## RQ-003 The three principles live on the home page only

Status: agreed

The About page is short on purpose: why the lab exists, one plain sentence about AI-assisted development, and contact.

## RQ-004 Nothing private is published

Status: agreed

The repository and the site are public. No hostnames, IPs, emails, credentials, filesystem paths, or personal details
appear anywhere, including the FAQ corpus and the specifications.

## RQ-005 Specifications are openly licensed

Status: agreed

Specifications under `site/spec/` are CC BY 4.0; everything else is all rights reserved. [LICENSE](../../LICENSE) is
the canonical statement of the terms.
