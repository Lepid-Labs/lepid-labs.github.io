# Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we get about Lepid Labs and about Weft, kept as a small documentation graph so that every answer can point at the one it depends on.

## How to read this

- **[About Lepid Labs](lepid-labs.md)** — who we are, what we make, how to reach us.
- **[About Weft](weft.md)** — what it is, how to run or embed it, licensing, status.
- **[About this site](this-site.md)** — how the FAQ and Docs pages are built, and why they look the way they do.

Use the document list on the left to move between sections, or press `Cmd+K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows and Linux) to search every question and heading at once. Links between answers are edges in the graph; the panel on the right, where shown, lists what links here and what this links to.
