# About Lepid Labs

## What is Lepid Labs?

A small software studio building developer tools for teams that keep their knowledge in the repository. The public product is [Weft](weft.md#what-is-weft); a set of smaller libraries for code intelligence, knowledge storage, and agent orchestration sits behind it and surfaces as products when ready.

## What does the name mean?

*Lepid* comes from Lepidoptera, the order of butterflies and moths. The mark is a stylised pair of wings. Nothing deeper than that.

## Is the work open source?

Weft is [MIT licensed](weft.md#how-is-weft-licensed) and developed in the open on GitHub. Other work is private while it is being shaped; anything useful on its own and safe to share follows the same path Weft did.

## How do I get in touch?

Through GitHub. The organisation is [github.com/Lepid-Labs](https://github.com/Lepid-Labs); questions and bug reports about Weft belong in [its issue tracker](https://github.com/Lepid-Labs/weft/issues). There is no mailing list and no support contract.

## Do you take on client work?

Not at the moment. The lab's time goes into its own tools.

## Can I use your design system?

The visual style on this site is `luminous-precision`, one theme from [ui-std-lib](https://github.com/nazuraki/ui-std-lib), which is MIT licensed and published to npm as `@nazuraki/styles`. See [About this site](this-site.md#why-does-everything-look-like-this) for how it is applied here.
