About

A software lab.

Every professional keeps a list of tools they would rebuild if they had the time. Lepid Labs is what happens when a working engineer finally has it. We build the things on that list, one at a time, small enough to finish, and release the ones that turn out to be useful to someone else.

We use AI-assisted development heavily. That is what makes a portfolio this wide possible for a lab this small. It is a fact about how we work, not a feature of what we make: every tool has to stand on its own, and the method is the same either way.

The first release is Weft, a documentation graph browser. It is MIT licensed and developed in the open; anything else that is useful on its own and safe to share follows the same path. What is coming after it is listed on the home page.

Contact

The organisation lives at github.com/Lepid-Labs. Questions and bug reports about Weft go to its issue tracker; the FAQ answers the common ones.
